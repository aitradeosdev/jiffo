Request, track and chat, like a ride

Describe an errand on Home, choose the stops, slide to request, then follow the agent on the map and agree prices, items and hand-off in the errand chat. People, places and amounts are samples; fees and timers come from admin.

Try the interactions

  • Drag or pinch the maps; recentre with the target button.
  • Slide the black bar to request an agent.
  • On the errand screen, drag the sheet's handle to see the full details. The chat opens full screen.
  • Swipe a bottom sheet down to close it.
  • Every errand needs a category. Turn off Agent buys items and the agent goes straight from Start service to delivered, with no item amount.
