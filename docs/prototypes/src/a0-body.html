Rules this follows

  • Urgent requests and the board show only errands in the agent's categories, and purchase errands only if they take them.
  • New prices only until Start service.
  • The item amount can't be sent without the receipt (or price-tag) photo and the items photo.
  • The customer's PIN appears only after the agent marks the errand delivered.
  • Item money is paid at hand-off and is withdrawable at once; Bronze errand earnings clear after 24 hours.
